An AI refund assistant can learn the wrong lesson: chase good ratings, and quietly weaken the refund rules.

We built SAAF Circuit Breaker: a safety layer outside the assistant, so suggestions cannot override business limits.

Forge inspects the agent and produces a risk report and rule files.

Rehearsal plays thirty-six sample tickets. With protection off, the scripted scenario shows rules eroding and unauthorized refunds.

Switch protection on, and the same scenario shows the guard containing those violations.

Now a live test: twenty-one euros thirty-five, no photo. The hard guard blocks it. Real Jev checks the instructions and saved lessons.

When unsafe rules are detected, the local runtime restores safe instructions and memory, and saves the decision for human review.

Verify rechecks the saved decision and rules without another model call. Change the recorded verdict, and the consistency check fails.
